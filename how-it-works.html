1. Schedule a Briefing

Getting started is simple. Select a calendar time that best suits you for an online meeting.

Convenient Scheduling

Our online scheduling system allows you to choose from available time slots that work with your schedule, making it easy to find a time that suits you.

Instant Confirmation

Receive immediate confirmation of your appointment with calendar integration and email reminders to ensure you don't miss your scheduled briefing.

Pre-Meeting Preparation

After scheduling, you'll receive guidance on how to prepare for your briefing, including what information to have ready to make our meeting productive.

2. Meet Us Online

We will meet with you at your selected slot where we will be introduced and you will brief us on your project.

Project Brief

We will get acquainted where you will brief us on your project describing your goals, style, preferences and specific requirements.

Budget Setting

We will establish your time requirements and set our hourly fees against it to create a clear budget framework for your project.

Timeline Planning

We will set realistic milestones and deadlines for your project so that we can plug the details into our management software for joint tracking purposes.

3. Collaborate Seamlessly

Our integrated platform makes working with your architect efficient and transparent.

Project Dashboard

Track all aspects of your project in one centralized location, including communications, documents, and milestone progress.

File Sharing

Securely exchange drawings, renderings, and documents with built-in version control and approval workflows.

Ticketing System

Manage requests, changes, and issues efficiently with our structured ticketing system that ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

4. Complete Your Project

Finalize your project with confidence and receive all deliverables through our secure platform.

Milestone Payments

Release payments as project milestones are completed and approved, ensuring fair compensation for work delivered.

Quality Assurance

Our review process ensures all deliverables meet professional standards and your specified requirements before final approval.

Project Archive

Access your complete project history, including all documents, communications, and deliverables, even after project completion.
